## Presentation Brief & Assessment Task

In this group project, your team will design, develop, and present a Python-based software application that addresses a real-world problem described in the given scenario. The scenario will be provided by the tutor and will include the design and development of a software solution.

### Assessment Task

You will receive a scenario that outlines a software development challenge in a university learning-resource context. Based on the scenario, your group must:

#### 1. Design a Software Solution

Your group should:

- analyse the scenario and identify user requirements;
- conduct mock stakeholder interviews to clarify system needs;
- identify functional and non-functional requirements;
- design an appropriate software architecture;
- design and implement a relational database schema;
- develop a Python 3 application that solves the given problem and interacts effectively with the database;
- include simple analytics and visualisation features to support decision-making.

Your system should include appropriate database tables, such as users, resources, bookings, departments, and booking statuses. You may modify or extend this structure based on your requirement analysis.

#### 2. Collaborate as a Development Team

Your group should work as a development team throughout the project.

You are expected to:

- divide tasks fairly among group members;
- use version control such as Git or GitHub;
- use project management tools or shared planning documents;
- document each member’s contribution;
- follow good coding practice;
- write readable and maintainable code;
- provide evidence of testing and debugging;
- prepare clear user and technical documentation.

Your final submission should show that the work was developed collaboratively rather than as disconnected individual parts.

#### 3. Apply and Reflect on a Software Development Lifecycle

Your group must select and apply an appropriate software development lifecycle methodology.

You may choose, for example:

- Agile;
- Waterfall;
- Iterative development;
- Prototyping;
- Scrum-inspired development.

You should explain why your chosen methodology is suitable for this project.

Your group should also critically evaluate the development process by discussing:

- how requirements were gathered and refined;
- how tasks were planned and allocated;
- how design decisions were made;
- how testing was carried out;
- what challenges occurred;
- how the group responded to problems;
- what could be improved in a future version.

#### 4. Use Test-Driven Development

You are expected to adopt a test-driven development approach during the project.

This means your group should:

- write test functions before implementing major features where possible;
- ensure each new function or feature is supported by one or more automated tests;
- test database operations such as create, read, update, and delete;
- test important Python functions;
- test analytics outputs;
- record evidence of testing in your documentation or slides.

You may use testing tools such as `unittest` or `pytest`.

#### 5. Deliver a Live Presentation and Demonstration

Your group must prepare and deliver a live presentation and software demonstration.

The presentation should include:

- the problem context and stakeholder needs;
- requirement analysis;
- proposed software architecture;
- database design, including ER diagram;
- Python application structure;
- key system features;
- CRUD functionality;
- analytics and visualisation outputs;
- evidence of test-driven development;
- software development lifecycle approach;
- group collaboration process;
- ethical, privacy, and usability considerations;
- limitations and future improvements.

Your group should also demonstrate the working prototype during the presentation. The demonstration should show how users can add, view, update, delete, search, analyse, and visualise booking records. You should be prepared to respond to questions about your design decisions, implementation

## Assessment Parameters & Policies

- **Duration**: 20 minutes (15 minutes + 5 minutes Q&A)
- **AI Scale**: **AI COLLABORATION** *(selected with ✓)*
  - **AI PLANNING** *(not selected)*: You may use AI for planning, idea development, and research. Your final submission should show how you have developed and refined these ideas.
  - **AI COLLABORATION** *(✓ selected)*: You may use AI to assist with specific tasks such as writing Python code, drafting text, refining, and evaluating your work. You must critically evaluate and modify any AI-generated content you use.
  - **FULL AI** *(not selected)*: You may use AI extensively throughout your work either as you wish, or as specifically directed in your assessment. Focus on directing AI to achieve your goals while demonstrating your critical thinking.
  - **AI EXPLORATION** *(not selected)*: You should use AI creatively to solve the task.
- **AI Policy**: Unauthorised and undisclosed use of Artificial Intelligence may result in a finding of Academic Misconduct and an academic sanction under Regulation 11. Guidance for WMG student on the use of Artificial Intelligence can be found here.

  - This work is subject to Peer Adjustment, which is a mechanism for ensuring every student is allocated a unique mark in respect of their contribution to the outcome of the assessment.   - Students who fail to submit their Peer Adjustment by the deadline forfeit 20% of the assessment mark.
